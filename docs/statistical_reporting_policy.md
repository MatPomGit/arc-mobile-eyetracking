# Statistical reporting policy

- Define coordinate frame and error units for every accuracy metric.
- Report accuracy and precision separately.
- Report valid-sample fraction, tracking-loss rate and calibration failure rate alongside error among valid samples.
- Report participant/run and device-level distributions before relying on pooled averages.
- Preserve failed/invalid observations with reason codes.
- Keep calibration targets separate from held-out evaluation targets.
- Report robustness conditions such as viewing distance, head pose and illumination separately when prespecified.
- Distinguish within-device validation, cross-device generalization and external-reference agreement.
- Use uncertainty intervals appropriate to the sampling unit and repeated-measures structure.
- Label protocol-defined, exploratory and post hoc analyses explicitly.
- Do not interpret a null difference as equivalence without an equivalence/non-inferiority design.

# Negative, null and boundary-condition findings

Record valid findings such as failed calibration, poor device support, high tracking-loss rate, unacceptable head-pose sensitivity, illumination failure regions, cross-device instability or weak agreement with an external reference.

Each entry should identify the evidence ID, estimator/calibration/protocol versions, analysis artifact, quality limitations and consequence for the publication or supported-device envelope.

Do not remove failed participants/devices/samples after inspecting error merely to improve reported accuracy. A null difference is not equivalence without an equivalence/non-inferiority design.
